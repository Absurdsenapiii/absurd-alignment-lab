# Milestones Arc

This folder is the long term archive of major financial and symbolic commitments made during the AbsurdSenapiii project.  
It is not a flex archive.  
It is a historical record for worldbuilding, accountability, and future partner verification.

Each milestone represents a decision point, a belief checkpoint, or a commitment that shaped the research lab and the persona.

---

## Purpose of This Archive
- Provide a permanent, chronological history of real world actions behind the lore  
- Allow future readers, partners, and institutions to understand the evolution of the project  
- Maintain transparency in a controlled, non public environment  
- Document symbolic anchors that define each era of the AbsurdSenapiii universe
