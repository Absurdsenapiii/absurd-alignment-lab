## Correction to historical claims

Earlier project-authored reports conflated founder-held assets and estimates with assets of AbsurdSenapiii Labs Pte. Ltd. In particular, the historical **S$1.26 million** and **S$2.1 million** figures did **not** represent company-owned assets or funds raised by Labs. A proposed transfer of founder-held assets to Labs was **not completed**, and the company treasury described in those reports was not established as presented.

Labs had no revenue. Anime-card sales were conducted through the separate AbsurdSenapiii sole proprietorship, rather than through Labs. Revenue and profit amounts previously attributed to Labs should not be relied on as company financial statements.

The founder clarification does **not** supply a replacement balance sheet, verify archived claims of zero liabilities, establish that any BUNI/Soar contractual matters or other obligations have been resolved, or confirm that company strike-off has been completed.
