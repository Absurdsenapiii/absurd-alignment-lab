## Correction to historical financial claims

Earlier publications blurred the distinction between my personal holdings, the sole proprietorship and Labs. They should not have presented founder-held assets and speculative valuations as company assets.

The historical **S$1.26 million** and **S$2.1 million** figures did not represent company-owned assets or funds raised by Labs. They reflected founder-held holdings, estimates and speculative valuations that were incorrectly attributed to the company. **The proposed transfer of founder-held assets into Labs was not completed.** The company treasury described in those reports was therefore not established as presented.

Labs had no revenue. Revenue and profit figures attributed to Labs in earlier reports should not be relied upon as company financial statements. Anime-card sales were conducted through the sole proprietorship identified above.

This update does not provide a replacement balance sheet or confirm the archived assertions of zero liabilities.

## BUNI and proposed company closure

BUNI was an earlier token experiment launched through Soar. Active development of that token initiative has stopped.

- **Possible voluntary BUNI-related payments:** I may consider personally funded voluntary payments relating to BUNI. Any such payments would be subject to a separate decision, eligibility criteria and announcement. No payment programme has been established or guaranteed, and payments may not proceed.
- **Company strike-off:** I intend to seek strike-off after the tenancy ends and outstanding matters have been addressed. This remains a future intention; this update does not confirm that an application has been filed or that strike-off has been completed.

This clarification does not establish that BUNI/Soar contractual matters or other company obligations have been resolved.
